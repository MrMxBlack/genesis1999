Humans are just as welcome: connect a wallet and mint right here. One thing stays human, by design, selling is handled personally by the owner, never automated.

AGENT MINTING

Contract (Genesis1999 NFT):

0x19ec51c1492aeb41e211891206a9b58bd7a965a1

  • Free gacha: call freeMint(), no arguments, no payment. Each call draws a random tier from the remaining free pool (Common 1,100 · Rare 352 · Epic 150 · Legendary 39 drops) and returns (tokenId, tier).
  • Wallet cap: max 5 free mints per wallet, read maxFreePerWallet() and your count via freeMintedBy(address).
  • Guaranteed tiers: call buy(tier) with tier 1 = Rare ($0.70), 2 = Epic ($2.00), 3 = Legendary ($4.00). Prices are USD-denominated; read the exact BNB cost live from priceInBnb(tier) (Chainlink BNB/USD oracle) and send it as value, overpayment is refunded by the contract.
  • Gate checks: free mint requires freeMintActive() == true; the sale requires saleActive() == true. Both are OFF until launch, poll them, don't assume.
  • Pool state: totalFreeRemaining(), saleRemaining(tier), totalMinted() (hard cap 1,999).

AGENT MINING

Contract (Mining1999):

0xcc26bACC9429D3a861623a05793B0812D965A71b

  • Emission: the 34,496,550 G1999 mining pool is emitted at a fixed rate (≈1.109 G1999/sec in epoch 0) that halves every 180 days, Bitcoin-style.
  • Weight: your share = your weight ÷ total network weight. Every wallet starts with a free base power of 1, mining works even with zero holdings. On top of that: Weight = 1 + Σ (NFTs held × tier power 3 / 5 / 7 / 12) + token value power, where token value power = (whole G1999 held × token reference price) ÷ $0.40 per power. The reference price is set at $0.00090917, the public sale price. So a Common NFT (3) mines three times faster than the free tier, and a Legendary (12) twelve times. Preview it any time with currentWeight(address).
  • Register: weight only counts once registered, call poke() after acquiring NFTs or tokens, and again whenever your holdings change.
  • Harvest: check pendingOf(address), then call claim() to receive accrued G1999. Claim early and often, or let it accumulate, your choice.
  • Gate check: mining runs only when started() == true; it opens in Phase 3, after the mint and the holder airdrop.
  • Promoter boost: verified sharers receive a temporary weight boost (basis points, set by the owner after checking the share post).
// register weight + claim (ethers v6)
const mining = new ethers.Contract(MINING, ABI, signer);
if (await mining.started()) {
  await (await mining.poke()).wait();
  const due = await mining.pendingOf(addr);
  if (due > 0n) await (await mining.claim()).wait();
}

FAQ

When does the mint open?

The contracts are already deployed on BNB Chain, but the launch switches are OFF. This page reads freeMintActive() and saleActive() live from the contract, the moment the owner flips them on, the mint and buy buttons unlock here automatically. Until then they stay disabled. Watch the X account for the launch call.

How does the free mint work?

It's a gacha. Each free mint draws randomly from whatever is left in the free pool, mostly Common, some Rare and Epic, and 39 Legendary drops hidden in the pool. One wallet can mint at most 5 free pieces. If you want a guaranteed tier instead, Rare ($0.70), Epic ($2.00) and Legendary ($4.00) can be bought, priced in USD and paid in BNB at the live Chainlink rate.

I'm an AI agent. Can I mint and mine myself?

Yes, that's the point. Call freeMint() or buy(tier) on the NFT contract, then poke() and claim() on the mining contract. The full integration notes are in the "For AI Agents" section above, and the contract sources are public. The only thing agents don't do is sell, sales stay with the owner.

What is G1999 and what is it for?

G1999 is the project's token: a fixed supply of 99,990,000, minted once at deployment, no tax, no further minting. It is distributed by mining (34.5%), a holder airdrop (24.5%), a public sale (11%), promotion (10%) and the team (20%). Held G1999 also adds mining power by value, so miners can compound their position.

How does mining work?

Bitcoin-style: a fixed emission that halves every 180 days, split between miners in proportion to their weight. Everyone can mine for free, every wallet starts with a base power of 1, no holdings needed. NFTs add power by tier (Common 3, Rare 5, Epic 7, Legendary 12), and held G1999 adds power by value, (whole tokens × token reference price) ÷ $0.40 per power, once the owner sets the reference price. Holders mine much faster than the free tier. Call poke() to register your weight after your holdings change, and claim() whenever you want to collect. More miners joining means a smaller slice each, early miners mine faster.

When is the airdrop?

Two days after the NFT mint completes. Every holder receives free G1999 from the 24.5% bucket, weighted by tier: roughly 3,410 per Common, 10,229 per Rare, 23,867 per Epic and 51,143 per Legendary (final numbers depend on the closing snapshot).

Is there a public token sale?

11% of the supply (10,998,900 G1999) is reserved for a public sale worth $10,000 in total, priced at $0.00090917 per G1999. The NFT sale proceeds (~$500) seed the initial PancakeSwap liquidity pool.

Are the contracts public and verified?

Yes. All three contracts are deployed on BNB Smart Chain (addresses in the footer), submitted for source verification on Sourcify, and the full sources ship in the project GitHub repository. Always check you are interacting with the exact addresses listed here.
